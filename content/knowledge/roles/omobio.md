## What I owned

At Omobio, Adisha developed and maintained large-scale React Native mobile apps for Dialog Axiata, serving millions of users on iOS and Android.

He played a key role in designing and developing the Dialog O2A Retailer App, a fully digital onboarding solution that streamlined retailer operations and reduced manual paperwork. He also built and maintained Appium-based automated testing frameworks for release stability and cross-platform quality.
