## What I owned

At Singapore Airlines, Adisha was part of the Manage Booking team, which designed and developed the Manage Booking frontend platform. The platform supports millions of customer transactions a year across global markets. This was team work.

He implemented Redux-based state management for complex, multi-step booking workflows with real-time data synchronisation. He integrated the frontend with AWS cloud services and Node.js backend APIs, keeping it available and fast under peak traffic. He also ran technical design reviews and effort estimation to support sprint planning and delivery.

## How Adisha joined Singapore Airlines

Adisha joined Singapore Airlines on a contract through ITCAN Pte Ltd, because direct hiring was frozen during the COVID period.

## Team and setup

The Manage Booking team had around 14 people, including about five frontend developers. Adisha was a Senior Software Engineer on the frontend team. He also did full-stack work, including on the backend-for-frontend layer.

The stack included React, TypeScript, JavaScript, Redux, the Context API, Material UI, Storybook, Node.js and AWS.

The team released quarterly.

## Shared React component library

When Adisha joined, Singapore Airlines had no reusable component library. He proposed starting one for multiple teams to use, then worked with the team to build it as a reusable React component library using React.js and TypeScript, with Storybook for developing and showcasing the components. The goal was to standardise UI patterns and make development more consistent across teams.

It started as a small component library repository. By the time Adisha left, it had grown into a shared resource used by most frontend teams at Singapore Airlines.
