# TGS (New Zealand) — Client work at MindLogics

Live at https://www.tgs.co.nz/

## What it is

TGS is the tour-booking website for Taniwha Guiding Services, a New Zealand operator offering multilingual guided day tours and transfers. It appears in Adisha's portfolio as a client project of MindLogics, where he worked. Whether Adisha worked on TGS himself isn't confirmed. The stack includes Next.js, React, AWS Lambda and Amazon CloudFront.
