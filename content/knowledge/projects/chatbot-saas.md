# AI Lead-Capture Chatbot — Adisha's SaaS in development

Status: in development · Demo on request

## What it is

The AI Lead-Capture Chatbot is Adisha's own SaaS product, still in development. It is an embeddable AI chatbot that businesses add to their website to engage visitors and capture qualified leads. The stack includes React, JavaScript, Node.js, Express.js and PostgreSQL.
